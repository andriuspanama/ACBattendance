What is in this zip
  1_i_pagrindini_aplanka/   8 files  -> the repo's main (top) folder
  2_i_github_workflows/     6 files  -> the repo's .github/workflows folder
  3_i_tools/tools/          2 files  -> a new "tools" folder in the repo

Files that already exist in the repo are replaced; the rest are new.
Your current data (data/ folder) is not touched.


STEP 1 – Main folder (8 files)
  1. Open https://github.com/andriuspanama/ACBattendance
  2. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files"
  3. Drag in ALL files from 1_i_pagrindini_aplanka
  4. Click "Commit changes"


STEP 2 – Workflow files (6 files)   <- this is what was missing last time
  1. In the repo, click the folder ".github", then "workflows"
     (you must be INSIDE that folder)
  2. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files"
  3. Drag in ALL files from 2_i_github_workflows
  4. Click "Commit changes"
  Check: the workflows folder must now contain capacity.yml, official.yml, pol.yml, gre.yml
  (plus bbl.yml, bsl.yml and your old ones).


STEP 3 – Tools folder (2 files, optional helper for filling the BSL sheet)
  1. Back on the repo's main page: "Add file" -> "Upload files"
  2. Open 3_i_tools and drag the folder "tools" itself (not the files) into the upload box
  3. Click "Commit changes"


STEP 4 – Google Sheets "Arenas" tab (Israeli venue names + capacities)
  Do NOT re-import the whole tab (it would undo your coordinate fixes).
  1. Open 4_i_Google_Sheets/Winner_League_G243-H257.xlsx, select cells A1:B15, copy
  2. In the Google Sheet, tab "Arenas", click cell G243 (Capacity of "Menora Mivtachim Arena")
  3. Paste. Rows 243–257 (Menora … HaKiriya) now have the league's capacities and the
     venue names basket.co.il uses (e.g. "The Shell, Be'er Sheva").
  Check: G250 (Conch Arena) = 3009.


STEP 5 – Run everything once (otherwise it starts by itself within 6 hours)
  Repo -> "Actions" tab -> for each workflow below: click it -> "Run workflow" -> "Run workflow"
    a) "Atnaujinti lankomumą (oficialios lygų svetainės)"   (official.yml – NBL, Korisliiga, LEBL, ABA, LBA, Israel)
    b) "Atnaujinti PLK lankomumą (Lenkija)"
       "Atnaujinti GBL lankomumą (Graikija)"
       "Atnaujinti BSL lankomumą (Turkija, rankiniu būdu)"
    c) when a) and b) are finished (green or red):
       "Perskaičiuoti arenų talpas"                           (capacity.yml – capacity, filling rate, main arenas)
  Then open https://andriuspanama.github.io/ACBattendance/ (refresh with Ctrl+F5).
